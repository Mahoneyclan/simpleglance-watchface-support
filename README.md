# SimpleGlance Watch Face support

## About the app

SimpleGlance is a clean, minimal digital watch face for Garmin fenix 6, fenix 7 and fenix 8 Solar watches.

- Large two-tone time, date, battery arc, heart rate and notification count.
- Two data fields of your choice: steps, calories, distance, floors, active minutes or elevation.
- Colours and fields are set in the Garmin Connect app; no code editing needed.
- Available in the Garmin Connect IQ Store.

## Get help

Questions, problems or ideas? Email **[mahoneyclanapps+simpleglance_watchface@gmail.com](mailto:mahoneyclanapps+simpleglance_watchface@gmail.com)**.

If you're reporting a problem, please include:

- what you were doing and what happened
- your watch model and its software version (on the watch: Settings → System → About)
- the app version (shown in the Connect IQ Store or the Garmin Connect app)
- a screenshot, if it helps

**Please note:** I'm a novice developer and make this app as a hobby, in my spare time. I'm happy to help and I read every message, but replies and fixes may take a while, and some problems may be beyond what I can fix.
