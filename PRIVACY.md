# Privacy Policy — SimpleGlance Watch Face

**Last updated: 31 October 2026 (applies to version 2.0.0)**

## Overview

SimpleGlance Watch Face ("the app") is a Connect IQ watch face that shows the time, date, battery level, heart rate, phone notification count and two activity stats you choose. This policy explains what data the app reads and what it does with it.

**In short: everything stays on your watch.** The app has no network access, sends nothing anywhere, and saves nothing of its own.

## Data the app reads

The app reads the following from your watch only to draw it on screen, each time the face updates (about once a minute):

| Data | Source on the watch | Shown as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heart rate | Live workout reading, or the watch's last one-minute heart-rate sample | HR panel (bpm) |
| Phone notification count | Device settings | Notification panel |
| Battery level and estimated days remaining | System stats | Battery arc and optional label |
| Steps, calories, distance, floors climbed, active minutes | Daily activity totals | Bottom row (only the two fields you choose) |
| Elevation | Barometric sensor history | Bottom row (only if you choose it) |
| Time and date | Watch clock | Time and date |

Heart rate is health data. The app only displays it; it does not record, store or transmit it.

## Data the app stores

Only your display settings (colours, chosen fields, 12/24-hour, arc label). These are kept by Garmin's standard watch-face settings system on your watch and in the Garmin Connect app. The app stores no other data.

## Data the app does not collect or transmit

- No location or GPS data
- No personal identification information
- No device identifiers or serial numbers
- No usage analytics or telemetry
- No data is sent to the developer or any third party

## Permissions

The app requests one Connect IQ permission, **SensorHistory**, used only to read the latest elevation reading. It does not request network (Communications), location (Positioning) or background-service permissions.

## Third-party services

None.
